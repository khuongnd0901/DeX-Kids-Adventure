## Vehicles
- Primary **yellow bus** (animated wheels) remains the tour vehicle.
- Red passenger car; yellow taxi; teal delivery truck; blue minibus; green scooter; blue bicycle.
- These are **fictional background traffic sprites**, not live traffic from GPS, not simulated collision behavior.
- Distinct roadside/road sprites should be positioned deterministically by chunk index and never imply real road traffic.
- All new art is project-authored SVG vector source, with no third-party artwork copied or embedded.

Artwork template generated externally is a **concept direction only** and is not shipped in the game. Test and approve colors, proportions, frame alignment and smartphone GPU memory before release.
