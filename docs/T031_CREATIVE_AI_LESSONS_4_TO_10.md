## What "unlimited" means
There is **no finite catalog of allowed questions, story ideas, riddles, topics or English words** for this creative mode. A verified provider may author another 15–20-card pack on a new trip and when the current pack is exhausted. It does **not** mean unlimited API traffic, billing, token consumption or infinite content in a single response. All physical requests, including the second-stage quality review and retries, use the existing persisted **5 starts/rolling 60 seconds and >=12.1s spacing**. Provider model/API daily and billable quotas still apply.

T-030's curated 96 quiz/80 word catalog and all offline entertainment remain unchanged and available as fallback. The T-016 POI-specific fact-index generation remains separate: AI cannot invent fact claims from GPS or assert the car has arrived somewhere.

## Parent setup
1. On the same Fold3 DeX monitor: Cài đặt → Độ tuổi bài học → select Sâu and Ong independently (each 4–10; defaults Sâu 5, Ong 4). BOTH distributes turns Sâu, Ong, BOTH; the shared turn uses the younger age.
2. AI Kids → configure Gemini/Groq key, specific model and verified Free Tier/quota, then enable **AI tự sáng tạo nội dung mới · 4–10 tuổi**. This is an **independent explicit opt-in, OFF by default**; it also enables automatic question packs.
3. Start a NEW trip. The app plays last-good content while generating a new pack asynchronously. If there is no saved AI pack, use curated/offline activities immediately. If AI or its reviewer fails, keep the prior pack and retry at most five additional times; refill on exhaustion, not every render frame.
4. Parent can revoke automatic generation/AI on the F10 menu during the trip, or disable creativity in settings and delete saved packages. Cloud child transcript sharing is separately consented and remains OFF unless independently enabled.

## Creative lesson contract
- Model authors **15–20** original items across >=4 topic labels and >=3 of QUIZ, STORY, RIDDLE, ENGLISH. At least two actual STORY items are explicitly fictional, including a spoken Vietnamese fiction label in the prompt. Each item has a question/short setup, a direct answer/story ending, a simple English word, its Vietnamese meaning, content for an explicit parent-owned age/focus schedule. The app assigns focus and exact target age by item index; the provider cannot change this metadata. The second review receives those authoritative ages.
- Ages 4–5: brief concrete one-idea sentences, no abstract or advanced concepts. Ages 6–7: basic comparisons and reasons. Ages 8–10: richer why/how questions, still short enough to hear in the car. Per-child age checked in the Java core validator before playback or persistence.
- Built-in deterministic checks reject malformed schema, non-age-matched focus, duplicates including a bounded recently heard hash history, too little topic/genre diversity, invalid text and selected unsafe/location/private-data phrases, including unaccented forms. Selected observed incorrect word/meaning pairs are also rejected; this is not a finite vocabulary allowlist. Mostly romanized packs and selected abstract scientific terms for ages 4–5 are rejected. These conservative checks do not prove language quality or factual correctness.
- **Independent AI quality stage** reviews at most four lessons per request and explicitly checks question-answer consistency, factual soundness, English translations and age/safety; if it rejects, keep last-good. Every batch must approve before any pack can be returned or saved. A16-card pack needs one authoring and four review calls (20cards need five review calls), all under the same5RPM limiter. The reviewer receives original indices, named lesson fields and authoritative ages, and returns approval plus at most five issue codes/indices and concise concrete reasons. Rejected, already Java-validated AI rows can inform the next bounded retry; they are never persisted as a playable pack. Approval with unresolved issues is rejected. The review stage **does not guarantee truth**. Known hard limits: facts outside the app's vetted catalog are not independently source-backed; translated words can be wrong; semantic near-duplicates may escape normalized-hash dedup; natural-language content filters cannot catch all unsuitable output. Adult supervision and content review are required.
- Never send GPS, location traces, captured child audio, raw child transcript, names or personal data to the creative-pack API. Age, non-identifying speaker enum and previous AI-authored questions are allowed to shape the lesson; API credentials stay in AndroidKeyStore.
- Independent AtomicFile cache per audience, bound to its age pair, SHA-256 integrity check, cursor and last-100 question hashes. Pack saved only after successful full validation and AI review; interruptions preserve previous pack.
